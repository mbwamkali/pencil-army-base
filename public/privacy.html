Privacy policy

Pencil Army Base, for the Android app and the website. Last updated 11 October 2026.

The short version

Pencil Army Base collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never sends anything you do over the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your game in progress, so you can carry on after closing the app.
  • Your choices on the Accessibility screen, such as larger text.
  • The game mode you picked last.

These are stored only on your phone or in your browser. Nobody else can see them, including us. Starting a new game clears the saved game, and uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data removes everything.

Sharing and copying

Save image and Copy layout only happen when you tap them. The picture or code goes to the app or place you choose with your phone's own share sheet or clipboard. The game does not see or keep where it went.

Permissions

The Android app uses vibration for a short buzz when a unit is destroyed. It asks for no other personal permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised on the game's GitHub issues page.
